Delivery & Collection Policy

Learn how PRICYN Bakery handles order collection, deliveries, wholesale requests and important delivery information before placing your order.

Last updated: July 2026 Accra, Ghana

01

Order Collection

Customers may collect confirmed orders from the agreed PRICYN Bakery branch at the agreed collection time. Please collect fresh bakery products promptly for the best quality.

02

Delivery Areas

Delivery is available within selected areas of Accra and surrounding communities, subject to driver availability and distance.

03

Delivery Hours

Delivery times are agreed during order confirmation. Times are estimates and may vary because of traffic, weather or production requirements.

04

Delivery Fees

Delivery charges depend on destination, order size and delivery requirements. Any applicable fee will be confirmed before your order is accepted.

05

Same-Day Orders

Same-day delivery or collection depends on product availability and production capacity. Early ordering is recommended.

06

Bulk & Wholesale Orders

Large, corporate and wholesale orders should be placed in advance to allow sufficient production time. Deposits may be required.

07

Customer Responsibilities

Please provide accurate contact details, delivery address and collection information. Incorrect information may delay fulfilment.

08

Missed Deliveries

If no one is available to receive an order at the agreed location, additional delivery charges or collection arrangements may apply.

09

Unexpected Delays

Unexpected delays caused by weather, traffic, equipment issues or other events beyond our control may affect delivery times.

10

Product Quality

Please inspect your order immediately after collection or delivery and contact us promptly if there is a problem.
