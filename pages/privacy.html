01

Overview

PRICYN Bakery is committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect information obtained through our website, our contact forms and our communication channels.

By using this website you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

02

Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with PRICYN Bakery, we may collect:

  • Your full name.
  • Your email address.
  • Your phone number.
  • Your enquiry or message.
  • Information relating to orders, wholesale enquiries, partnerships or delivery requests.
  • Technical information such as browser type and device information automatically supplied by your browser.
03

How We Collect Information

We collect information whenever you:

  • Complete our website contact form.
  • Send us an email.
  • Call or message us on WhatsApp.
  • Place an order.
  • Request wholesale information.
  • Interact with our social media pages.
04

How We Use Your Information

We use your information only for legitimate business purposes, including:

  • Responding to enquiries.
  • Processing orders.
  • Providing customer support.
  • Communicating about deliveries.
  • Improving our website and services.
  • Meeting legal obligations.
05

Sharing Your Information

PRICYN Bakery does not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

We may share limited information only when necessary to operate our website or provide our services, including:

  • EmailJS for contact form delivery.
  • Google Maps for location services.
  • WhatsApp when you choose to contact us.
  • Website hosting and technical service providers.
  • Government authorities where required by law.
06

Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to help protect your personal information from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or destruction.

While we take security seriously, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

07

Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion where legally appropriate.
  • Withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis.
  • Object to certain processing activities.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details at the end of this policy.

08

Cookies & Third-Party Services

Our website may use basic browser technologies and trusted third-party services to improve functionality and user experience.

These services may include:

  • Google Fonts
  • Google Maps
  • Font Awesome
  • EmailJS

Each third-party service operates under its own privacy policy.
