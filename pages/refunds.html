Returns & Refund Policy

Because our products are freshly baked and perishable, this policy explains when replacements, refunds or credits may be available.

Last Updated: July 2026Accra, Ghana

01

Fresh Bakery Products

Bread, pastries and other baked goods are freshly prepared and perishable. For food safety reasons, products cannot normally be returned simply because you changed your mind after collection or delivery.

02

Incorrect Orders

If you receive the wrong product, quantity or size, contact us immediately. After verification we will arrange an appropriate replacement, store credit or refund.

03

Damaged Products

If an order is damaged during collection or delivery, notify us promptly and provide photographs where possible.

04

Quality Concerns

If a product does not meet our normal quality standards, contact us on the same day you receive it so we can investigate fairly.

05

Cakes & Custom Orders

Custom cakes and personalised products are made specifically for each customer. Once production has started, cancellations or refunds may not be available except where required by law.

06

Refund Eligibility

  • Incorrect order supplied.
  • Product damaged before collection or delivery.
  • Verified production quality issue.
  • Confirmed order cannot be fulfilled.
07

Replacement Policy

Where appropriate, PRICYN Bakery may provide a replacement instead of a refund so customers receive fresh products as quickly as possible.
