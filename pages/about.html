Our Story

PRICYN Bakery was founded by Prince Osei Kwarteng, an accountant and entrepreneur who transformed his passion for baking into a growing business venture.

The name PRICYN combines the names Prince and Cynthia, reflecting the personal values and inspiration behind the brand.

Since its establishment, the bakery has remained committed to producing fresh bread and pastries using quality ingredients, skilled craftsmanship and a genuine commitment to customer satisfaction.
