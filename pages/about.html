OUR STORY

Built with passion,
baked with purpose.

PRICYN Bakery was founded by Prince Osei Kwarteng, an accountant and entrepreneur who transformed his passion for baking into a growing bakery brand.

The name PRICYN combines Prince and Cynthia, reflecting the personal values, family inspiration and care behind the business.

Today, PRICYN Bakery continues to serve fresh bread, pastries and bakery products made with quality ingredients, skilled hands and a genuine commitment to customer satisfaction.
